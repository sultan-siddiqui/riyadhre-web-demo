Chief Executive Officer

Fahad Al-Hesni

Chief Executive Officer

Fahad Al-Hesni

Bringing over 30 years of distinguished leadership in the insurance and reinsurance industry. He has played a pivotal role in shaping Saudi Arabia’s insurance landscape, notably as Managing Director and CEO of Saudi Re from 2011 to March 2024. Prior to joining Riyadh Re as the CEO, he served as Advisor to the Insurance Authority CEO, leading the National Insurance Strategy (NIS) and sector transformation initiatives. His strategic vision and extensive international experience, including board roles with Probitas Holdings (Bermuda) and Probitas 1492 Services Ltd., position him at the forefront of the Kingdom’s reinsurance evolution.

Chief Financial Officer

Nilmin Pieries

Chief Financial Officer

Nilmin Pieries

Nilmin has over 35 years of extensive experience in insurance and reinsurance across multiple global markets. He has held senior leadership roles in Saudi Arabia and internationally, including over a decade as CFO of Saudi Re, where he later served as Advisor to the Board of Directors. His deep financial acumen and strategic insight have been instrumental in driving growth, governance, and sustainable performance within the (re) insurance sector.

Chief Underwriting Officer

Belhassen Tonat

Chief Underwriting Officer

Belhassen Tonat

Belhassen has over 20 years of global reinsurance experience across four continents — MENA, Sub-Saharan Africa, Europe, and Asia Pacific. He has successfully led market expansion initiatives, developed innovative products, and navigated complex regulatory environments. With a proven ability to design and execute comprehensive underwriting strategies, he is driving Riyadh Re’s underwriting excellence and sustainable portfolio growth.

Chief Actuary and Chief Risk Officer

Mohamed Afify

Chief Actuary and Chief Risk Officer

Mohamed Afify

Bringing over 24 years of international experience in insurance and reinsurance. Before joining Riyadh Re, he was Executive Director and Head of Reserving for the UK and EMEA at AIG, where he modernized reserving practices and enhanced group-wide actuarial standards. Previously, he led international reserving at StarStone International and advised major insurers as Senior Manager at PwC UK. His expertise spans reserving, capital modeling, Solvency II, and strategic risk management, making him a key leader in actuarial governance and enterprise risk oversight.

Chairman of the Board

H.H. Prince Sultan bin Khalid bin Faisal Al Saud

Chairman of the Board

H.H. Prince Sultan bin Khalid bin Faisal Al Saud

H.H. Prince Sultan Bin Khalid Bin Faisal Al Saud has extensive leadership experience in industrial finance, credit, and governance. As CEO of the Saudi Industrial Development Fund (SIDF) and a Stanford MBA graduate, he has played a pivotal role in advancing Saudi Arabia’s industrial transformation. A former Aramco engineer and active member of national boards such as Monsha’at, he champions strong governance and strategic oversight in alignment with Vision 2030.

Vice Chairman of the Board

Jean-Luc Gourgeon

Vice Chairman of the Board

Jean-Luc Gourgeon

He is a seasoned Franco-global reinsurance executive who combines traditional underwriting discipline and governance excellence with innovation in risk analytics. Having held senior leadership roles at PartnerRe and Everest Re, as well as multiple board positions across Europe and the GCC, he brings deep international perspective. At Synomen, his focus is on integrating climate intelligence and satellite-based data to enhance agricultural and catastrophe risk modeling.

Board Member

Waleed AlHumaid

Board Member

Waleed AlHumaid

Brings over two decades of experience in finance and investment management. As CEO of Al Rajhi Capital, he has led the firm’s growth into one of Saudi Arabia’s leading Shariah-compliant financial institutions, overseeing record asset expansion and innovative fund offerings. His career spans key roles at Aramco, Jadwa Investment, and SNB Capital, and he currently serves on the board of the Gulf Investment Corporation.

Board Member

Dr. Kai-Uwe Schanz

Board Member

Dr. Kai-Uwe Schanz

Internationally recognized economist and thought leader in the reinsurance industry. He is the Co-Founder of Purpose for Insurance AG, a Swiss advisory firm advancing strategic positioning and stakeholder engagement for global (re)insurers. Previously, he served as Deputy Managing Director and Head of Research & Foresight at The Geneva Association, leading the Global Reinsurance Forum of top reinsurer CEOs. With earlier experience as Chief Economist for Swiss Re in Asia-Pacific, he brings exceptional insight into global insurance economics and strategic foresight.

Board Member

Abdulatif Ali Alseif

Board Member

Abdulatif Ali Alseif

Abdulatif Ali Alseif is a distinguished Saudi Arabian investment executive with more than two decades of leadership experience in asset management, financial strategy, and institutional investing. He serves as Chairman of The Saudi Investment Bank and is the Founder and CEO of Sabeen Investment Company, a CMA‑regulated asset manager. He previously led Ra’idah Investment Company, overseeing major public investment portfolios, and has held senior roles at Saudi Aramco, KAUST, MASIC, and Al Rajhi Bank. Mr. Alseif holds an MBA and an MA in Economics from Boston University, along with CPA and CFA designations, and is recognized for his expertise in strategic investments, multi‑asset portfolio management, and corporate governance across key sectors of the Saudi economy

Board Member

Dr. Peter Franz Hugger

Board Member

Dr. Peter Franz Hugger

Dr. Peter is a German-Swiss citizen with a doctorate degree from University of Konstanz (Germany). He brings more than 30 years of experience in the reinsurance industry, spanning underwriting, client relationship management, and senior executive leadership. His C level experience includes serving as CEO of Echo Reinsurance from 2012 to 2018, as well as Member of the Board of Directors of Saudi Re from 2020 to 2023. Since March 2025, he also serves as a Member of the Board of Directors of Stonefort Re (Luxembourg). He possesses extensive international market expertise, with particular depth in the MENA and APAC region.
